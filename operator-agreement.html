Operator Agreement

This is the agreement you accept at checkout. It sets out what the territory licence gives you, what it does not, and where our responsibility ends and yours begins — because the consignment business you run on this platform is yours, not ours.

You are buying software and a city. You are not buying a job, a franchise, or a share of our business.

The shop is yours to run. The stock, the pricing, the consignors, the customers, and the money are all yours — and so is the responsibility for them.

1. Parties and when this starts

This Operator Agreement is between Kingdom Creatives LLC, an Idaho limited liability company (“we”, “us”), and you, the person or business buying a ConsignmentBiz territory (“you”, the “Operator”).

It takes effect when you complete checkout and accept it, and it stays in force while your account is active. It sits alongside our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Refund Policy; where this Agreement is more specific about the operator relationship, this Agreement governs.

2. The territory licence

In exchange for the fee in section 3, we grant you a non-exclusive licence to use the ConsignmentBiz platform, together with an exclusive right to operate a ConsignmentBiz shop in one named city.

What the exclusivity covers

  • We will not sell or license that city to another ConsignmentBiz operator while your account is active.
  • Your claim is recorded at the moment your payment clears. Cities are allocated strictly in that order, and the record is the authority — not an email, a conversation, or an intention to buy.
  • It runs for as long as your account is active. There is no renewal, no expiry, and nothing further to pay to keep it.

What it does not cover

  • It is not a monopoly on consignment in your city. Other shops — on other platforms, on no platform, or with no website at all — can and will trade alongside you. We do not prevent that, police it, or compensate you for it.
  • It is one city, not a region. Not a county, not a metro area, not a radius. Neighbouring towns are separate territories and may be sold to someone else.
  • It is not a franchise, business opportunity, agency, partnership, or joint venture, and it grants no share in our business or revenue.
  • It does not restrict our own demonstration shop, which does not trade.

Non-transferable. You may not sell, assign, sublicense, rent, or gift your territory, and you may not operate it on behalf of an undisclosed third party. If you want to move the licence to a company you control, or to a buyer of your business, email us first — we will usually agree, in writing, but a transfer we have not approved is void.

Cities are matched on name and state. Where a place is commonly written more than one way, ask us before you buy and we will confirm which entry covers you.

3. The fee

The territory fee is $197, charged once. There is no monthly fee, no renewal, no per-listing charge, and no commission on anything you sell. We keep no card on file, and the platform has no mechanism to bill you again.

Refunds are governed by the Refund Policy: seven days, while the territory is still unused.

4. What we are responsible for

  • Hosting your shop and keeping the platform running;
  • Your public storefront, consignor intake form, operator dashboard, checkout, and transactional email;
  • Holding your city against other ConsignmentBiz operators;
  • Keeping your data secure and dealing with it as set out in the Privacy Policy.

We provide software. We do not provide training, mentoring, sales leads, marketing, or business advice, and none of those are included in the fee.

No earnings claim, ever. We make no representation, forecast, or guarantee about revenue, profit, sales volume, or how quickly you will find consignors. Nothing on our site, in our email, or in this Agreement should be read as one. What you earn depends on your market, your pricing, and your work.

5. What you are responsible for

You operate an independent consignment business. That means all of the following are yours:

  • Your legal entity — registering it, and keeping it in good standing;
  • Licensing — secondhand-dealer permits, resale certificates, and any local trading licence. Many jurisdictions regulate secondhand dealing specifically, and some require record-keeping on every item taken in;
  • Tax — sales tax registration, collection and remittance, and your own income tax. We do not calculate, collect, file, or remit any tax on your behalf, and the platform is not a tax engine;
  • Insurance — including cover for goods belonging to other people while they are in your care;
  • Premises and handling — storage, security, and safe collection arrangements;
  • The accuracy of every listing — condition, age, provenance, authenticity, measurements, and faults.

6. Your consignors

The agreement between you and a consignor is yours alone. We are not a party to it, do not draft it, and will not enforce or interpret it.

At minimum, settle in writing with each consignor: the share they receive, how long you will hold an item, what happens if it does not sell, who bears the risk of loss or damage while you hold it, and when and how you will pay them.

On the split. Throughout the platform, the percentage you set is the consignor’s share — the part paid out to them. Sixty percent means the consignor receives sixty and you keep forty. The dashboard records the amount owed at the moment of sale, so a later price change can never alter what someone is already owed.

That record is a bookkeeping aid, not an escrow. We never hold, route, or release your consignors’ money. Paying them is entirely your obligation, on whatever schedule you agreed with them.

You confirm that you have the right to list and sell every item you put on your shop, and that you have the consignor’s permission to publish photographs of their property.

7. Your buyers

Every sale is between you and your buyer. We are not the seller, the merchant of record for your goods, or a party to the transaction.

The platform offers two modes: card checkout for items that can be paid for online, and reserve-and-collect for pieces that need arranging. You choose per item.

Your own policies on returns, exchanges, damage in transit, collection windows, and disputes are yours to set and to honour. If a buyer has a complaint about an item, it is your complaint to resolve.

8. Conduct and prohibited stock

You will not use the platform to list or sell:

  • Stolen property, or anything you cannot prove you have the right to sell;
  • Firearms, ammunition, or weapons requiring a licensed transfer;
  • Controlled substances, tobacco, alcohol, or prescription items;
  • Live animals, human remains, or protected wildlife products — ivory and certain furs and feathers included;
  • Counterfeit goods, or replicas presented as authentic;
  • Recalled products, or electrical goods you know to be unsafe.

You will also not misrepresent an item, present the shop as ours rather than yours, send bulk unsolicited email through the platform’s transactional mail, or resell or white-label the platform itself.

9. Branding

Your shop carries your name, not ours. You may say truthfully that your shop is powered by ConsignmentBiz, and the platform credit appears in your footer.

You may not use our name or marks in a way that suggests we endorse your business, that you are our agent or employee, or that you speak for us. You may not register a domain, business name, or social account that could be mistaken for ours.

10. Your data and your customers’ data

Your listings, photographs, consignor records, and customer records are yours. We store and process them to run your shop, and for nothing else.

You are the controller of the personal information you collect from consignors and buyers. Telling them what you collect and why, honouring their requests about it, and complying with the privacy law where you trade are your obligations, not ours. The Privacy Policy sets out that division in full.

11. Term, suspension and termination

Term. This Agreement runs from checkout until either of us ends it.

You may leave at any time. Email us and we will deactivate the shop, and delete your data if you want it deleted. Leaving after the refund window does not entitle you to money back.

We may suspend or terminate for a serious or repeated breach of sections 5 through 9, for a chargeback filed against a used territory, or where the law requires it. Except where the activity is unlawful, we will contact you first and give you a fair chance to put it right.

On termination, your territory is released and returns to the available pool, and your shop stops being publicly reachable. Termination for breach carries no refund. We will give you a reasonable opportunity to export your records before deletion.

Dormancy. We will not reclaim a territory merely because it is quiet. If we ever introduce a minimum-activity requirement, it will not apply to a territory bought before it was announced.

12. Independent businesses

You are an independent business. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, agency, partnership, joint venture, or franchise relationship, and neither of us may bind the other or incur obligations in the other’s name.

You have full control over your own prices, hours, stock, staffing, and methods. We do not direct how you run the business, and we do not require you to buy anything else from us.

You will indemnify us against claims, losses and reasonable legal costs arising from the items you list, your dealings with consignors and buyers, your tax or licensing obligations, your breach of this Agreement, or your breach of the law.
